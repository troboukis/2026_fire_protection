# AntiNERO report findings audit

Review date: 7 September 2026. Archive cutoff: 12 August 2026.

All 47 research paragraphs were compared with archived contract and Diavgeia extracted texts. This is a document-content review, not a live registry refresh, visual PDF check, site inspection or comprehensive audit of every metadata field. Citations currently identify whole documents.

## Material corrections

- Aigaleo 24SYMV014192335: the 11 July 2025 acceptance was partial and excluded planting. Later records place planting maintenance completion on 30 March 2026.
- 25SYMV016570021: the fifteen-month maintenance rule has an express exception for works whose nature requires no maintenance.
- 23SYMV012820428: acceptance protocol dated 24 November 2023; approval dated 11 December 2023.

The study contract 24SYMV015170089 confirms EUR 799,483.29 excluding VAT / EUR 991,359.28 including VAT. The larger archived metadata amount is the financing budget, not that agreed fee.

## Unresolved questions

- 24SYMV014447969: early Megara acceptance needs reconciliation with the maintenance clause. The reviewed sources do not explain an applicable exception.
- 24SYMV014217833: the suggested maintenance explanation for completion timing is unproven; a certificate also contains a contract-date inconsistency.

Neither establishes a contractual breach. Negative search results, calculated deadlines, approval versus payment, office-specific scope and supplemental approval versus signed agreement are explicitly qualified in the review records.
